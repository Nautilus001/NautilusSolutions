# Nautilus Solutions

Nautilus Solutions is a software consultancy. This context is its public marketing site: a website-first brochure that explains the firm and captures leads.

## Language

**Nautilus Solutions**:
The firm this marketing site represents.
_Avoid_: Meridian Labs, Meridian, Nautilus Consulting

**Nautilus shell**:
The firm's primary mark — a stylized nautilus shell on a transparent ground — used with the Nautilus Solutions wordmark.
_Avoid_: Meridian chevron, generic app icon, boxed or backgrounded shell as the primary mark

**Marketing site**:
The public brochure (home, about, services, portfolio, contact). It is a website first, not a store-listed native app.
_Avoid_: app, portal, product

**Lead**:
A prospective client's inquiry submitted on the marketing site (name, email, message, optional company and service of interest).
_Avoid_: customer, ticket, contact (the page is Contact; the thing submitted is a Lead)

**Service**:
A packaged offering Nautilus Solutions sells.
_Avoid_: product

**Project**:
A past engagement shown on the portfolio page. Slots may exist before any real engagement is written.
_Avoid_: case study, work sample
